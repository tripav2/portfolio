Single Transistor Amplifier

A 2N3904 common-collector (emitter-follower) stage designed as a buffer: high input impedance, low output impedance, ~0.9 gain between a 100 Ω source and load. Midband gain, linearity, and low harmonic distortion verified in simulation and on the bench.
